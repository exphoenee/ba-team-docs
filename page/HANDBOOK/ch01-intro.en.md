# 1. About the Product

**BA Team** is a Claude AI-powered, specialized workflow system designed to make the business analysis phase of IT projects more efficient. The system provides a "virtual team" of AI agents, with you as the team leader.

## What BA Team Does

BA Team automatically produces **audit-ready, professional Business Analyst documentation** from raw project materials — emails, meeting notes, Word documents, Excel spreadsheets, and Outlook messages.

## Why It's Different from a Simple Chatbot

| Chatbot | BA Team |
|---|---|
| Each session starts from scratch | Long-term memory preserves decisions and context |
| All background must be provided manually | Automatically reads previous session results |
| No quality assurance | Won't generate documents with incomplete data |
| No standardized identifiers | FR-XXX, NFR-XXX, US-XXX, Q-XXX — everything is traceable |
| Can't handle Office files | Automatically converts .docx, .xlsx, .msg, .eml files |

## Core Principles

- **You're in charge**: AI does the technical work, strategic decisions remain yours
- **Audit-ready documentation**: All outputs are suitable for developer handover and regulatory audit
- **Never invents**: What the client didn't say, the AI won't put in the document
- **Never silently resolves conflicts**: Conflicts are flagged, and you decide
- **Full Hungarian language support**: All documents and feedback are in Hungarian
